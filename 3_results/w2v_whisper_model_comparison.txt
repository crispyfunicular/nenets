KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------

1. XLSR improves slightly on the fixed test after adding KhO data
   (WER 70.87% -> 68.42%, CER 16.47% -> 15.51%).

2. Whisper Small RU degrades sharply on the same fixed test
   (WER 72.82% -> 106.32%) despite looking better on its internal val split.
   Do not trust the internal val WER alone for this run.

3. XLSR remains the best system for Nenets under this protocol.


BOTTOM LINE
-----------
More annotated data helped the CTC model; it hurt Whisper Small RU on the
held-out historical test. Prefer the new XLSR checkpoint; treat the new
Whisper Small RU checkpoint as a failed transfer until diagnosed.
